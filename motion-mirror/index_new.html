Five short activities based on the standard Parkinson's motor exam and on physiotherapy research. You only need a webcam and about 3 minutes. There is no time pressure.

Hand tracking
Runs on your deviceAbout 3 minutesA demo, not a medical test

What you will do

1. Finger tapping

Tap your thumb and index finger together, big and fast, 10 times.

2. Tap with the music

Tap on each beat as the notes reach the line.

3. Steadiness

Hold your hand up, fingers spread, as still as you can for 10 seconds.

4. Light up the spiral

Draw along the spiral with your index finger to light up the stars.

5. Big movements

On each beat, open your hand wide until the flower reaches the ring.
