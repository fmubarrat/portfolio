Attune

A study prototype that adapts how an AI coding assistant engages novices, based on signals of over-reliance and struggle sensed on the device.

What you will do

Answer three quick questions, solve three short Python tasks, then answer the questions again and a short questionnaire (about 35 minutes). An AI assistant is available whenever you want help. Like any AI, its suggestions may be wrong. After each task you answer one short question about the code.

What is measured

  • Always: your interactions (runs, AI requests, insertions) and typing rhythm (key counts and pauses, never the keys themselves).
  • Only if you turn on the camera: head direction, brow movement and blink rate, computed in this browser from facial landmarks. No video, image or landmark is stored or sent anywhere.
Study setup (researcher)

These can be preset in the link, e.g. ?pid=P07&condition=adaptive&flawed=2&explain=1&cam=0, so participants never see this panel. For demos, open Researcher view (or press D) to watch the signals and the policy, and use “Simulate” to show each adaptation without acting it out.
